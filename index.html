How this lesson works

All strategies start with the same cash and receive the same deposit at the start of each month. Buy & hold invests the starting cash and holds later deposits as cash. Regular buying invests each deposit. Pullback buys available cash after a monthly decline of at least 5%, then sells after a rise of at least 10% from entry. Breakout buys above the previous three monthly prices, then sells on a decline of 8% from entry. Signals use a completed month and execute at the next month’s price. Every trade pays the selected fee. These simple rules illustrate mechanics; they are not STP backtests, predictions, or proof of an advantage.
